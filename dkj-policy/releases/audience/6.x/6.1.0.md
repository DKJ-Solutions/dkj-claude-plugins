# Release notes v6.1.0 🚀

**Date:** 2026-10-30\
**Type:** Minor\
**For whom:** consumers of this product

## What changed

### The connector session check is now called `consumer-sessioncheck`

Your session start prints its register lines under `consumer-sessioncheck` instead of
`connector-sessioncheck`, and the dispatcher's timing line reads `consumer`. **You only need to act if
something of yours matches the old name**: a filter, a note, or a script that reads the session-start
output. Update it to the new name. Otherwise, no action is needed.
([#3675](https://github.com/DKJ-Solutions/dkj-marketplace/issues/3675))

### Found a closed issue that came back? Comment on it and turn it into a record

When a session in your repo finds that the fix for a closed, *completed* issue did not hold, there is now
one route: comment on that issue and convert it into a record (`awaiting-recurrences`, `[RECORD]`, closed
as *not planned*). Do not file a new issue beside it. No action is needed until that happens.
([#3689](https://github.com/DKJ-Solutions/dkj-marketplace/issues/3689))

### The register checks run again beside a checkout named `dkj-marketplace`

If your machine's source checkout is a fresh clone named `dkj-marketplace`, your sessions run the
consumer registration, lens inventory and agent-def drift checks again. Before this release they skipped
those checks without saying so. No action is needed.
([#3666](https://github.com/DKJ-Solutions/dkj-marketplace/issues/3666))

### Your toolbox is easier to find your way around

- **SessionStart and the Clock are now behind a new Performance card** on the hub's home page, one click
  deeper than before.
- **The Installed plugins page groups your plugins by where they come from**: the dkj-marketplace
  plugins under one heading, and MCP plugins such as Figma under another.
- **A new AI card** opens Rules, Skills and Specialists. Specialists lists every lens your repo keeps,
  and you can read each one on its own page. Rules and Skills have no content yet.
- **Pages that group cards under a heading sit slightly tighter**, because the house style no longer adds
  extra space above a headed group.

No action is needed.
