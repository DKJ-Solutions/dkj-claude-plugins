# Release notes v5.20.0 🚀

**Date:** 2026-10-15\
**Type:** Minor\
**For whom:** consumers of this product, and colleagues in the organisation -- one section each

The house-style skill points at house-style-v3, with the brand book's renamed colour tokens

## What changed

**One thing to do in a DKJ-Solutions app, and only when it next takes a copy of the house style:
rename the colour tokens in your own layer.** Nothing changes until you take that copy.

### DKJ-Solutions repos: the house style is now `house-style-v3`

The `house-style` skill now fetches `house-style-v3` from `dkj-brandbook`, with new checksums
([#3033](https://github.com/DKJ-Solutions/dkj-claude-plugins/issues/3033)). v3 renames the colour tokens:

- `--good`, `--bad`, `--up`, `--down`, `--warn`, `--ink`, `--muted`, `--gain`, `--grid`, `--primary-soft`
  and the bare `--primary` are gone;
- their roles now sit on `--positive-N`, `--negative-N`, `--warning-N` and a nine-step `--primary-1` to
  `--primary-9`, where `--primary-9` is the text colour and `--primary-7` the highlight.

When your app takes the v3 copy, rename every old token in your own layer, or those rules lose their
colour without an error. The visible symptom is text, lines or accents that fall back to the browser
default. The mapping tables are in
[`house-style/README.md` at the tag](https://github.com/DKJ-Solutions/dkj-brandbook/blob/house-style-v3/house-style/README.md).

### Smaller changes, no action needed

- The connector register names the thumbnail generator's renamed repo and checkout folder
  ([#3028](https://github.com/DKJ-Solutions/dkj-claude-plugins/issues/3028)), registers it under
  `dkj-solutions` ([#3029](https://github.com/DKJ-Solutions/dkj-claude-plugins/issues/3029)), and no
  longer expects lenses in `djcylow-react` ([#3031](https://github.com/DKJ-Solutions/dkj-claude-plugins/issues/3031)).

## What it is worth

- **The first step towards one design system across every DKJ-Solutions app.** The brand book's work since
  v2 (the renamed tokens, the nine-step scale, the spacing and type scales) is now a release the apps can
  take, instead of something only the brand book itself showed. The per-app migrations follow, each as
  its own change. The gate that keeps them aligned afterwards is
  [#3035](https://github.com/DKJ-Solutions/dkj-claude-plugins/issues/3035).
- **What this release cost.** The clock started at 13:00:48. The cut (lint, all suites, bump, commit,
  tag, push) finished at 13:06:33, **5m 45s**, all of it blocking.

## What was still open at this release

The apps themselves had not moved yet. `dkj-lifehub` carried a vendored copy that matched neither v1 nor
v2. `dkj-recipes`, `dkj-maplestory-classic-mesowise`, `dkj-etf-tracker` and `dkj-music-library` had no
copy at all. The `check-house-style` gate (#3035) was filed and not yet built.
