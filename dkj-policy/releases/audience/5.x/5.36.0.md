# Release notes v5.36.0 🚀

**Date:** 2026-10-28\
**Type:** Minor\
**For whom:** consumers of this product

## What changed

**Your live Shopify theme is guarded however the push spells its flags.** The live-theme guard now also
blocks `shopify theme push` with the short flags: `-a` for `--allow-live`, `-l` or `--live`, and clusters
such as `-la`, plus a push naming the live theme with `-t <name> -a`. Those used to pass. A preview push and
every pull stay allowed. No action needed, but a session that pushes to live now has to carry the
authorisation marker whichever way it writes the command.

**Your hub can open in the same light/dark pair as your product.** `DefaultTheme` in `Get-DevPreview` now
also takes a pair, for example `@{ Light = 'blue'; Dark = 'sky' }`, so on a dark OS the hub no longer opens
light while your product opens dark. The single theme id still works unchanged. See the
[hub README](../../../../plugins/dkj-policy/hub/README.md).

**The hub is now called Toolbox on screen.** The session-start line opens with `Toolbox:`, the header tags
your repo `toolbox`, and the first crumb reads Tools. Your product's own pages keep Home, and the `/hub`
route and the `OwnHub` key are unchanged. No action needed.

**Each DKJ repo has a card and a page of its own under Connectors.** `/connectors/dkj` shows a card per
repo, three to a row, with its GitHub facts, its checkout and its connector status. Each card opens
`/connectors/dkj/<name>`, with that repo's GitHub facts, this machine's view of its checkout, its connector
report and the lenses it has filled in. The old Connected repos table, On GitHub grid and Issues card are
gone; their per-repo content is on these pages.

**Card titles stay on one line, and card groups get room to breathe.** In the house style a card title that
does not fit is cut off with an ellipsis instead of making the card taller than its neighbours. Every card
group (`.grid-2`, `.grid-3`, and the new `.card-grid` class for a grid of your own) keeps at least 12px of
padding round its cards, on a surface one step off the page. Your app picks this up with its next copy of
`house-style.css`.

**You can prepare a Stopwatch card before you start the run.** The new `stopwatch-prepare` skill
(`-Label "release cut"`, `-Remove` to withdraw it) puts a "Ready, not started" card on `/stopwatch`. The run
still starts only from a prompt whose first line is `stopwatch: <label>`, and lands on that card. No store
needs migrating.
